## Terminals

You are probably used to interacting with your computer using a mouse to point and click on buttons, your keyboard to type text, or your using touch gestures with your smartphone.

A long time ago - well, maybe not that long ago, the primary way to interact with a computer was to text it. Yes, text it, the same way you text your parents when it's their anniversary.
But instead of WhatsApp or Instagram, the texting app between you and your computer is called the **terminal**. The specific terminal app depends on your platform:

|Platform|Terminal App|
|---|---|
|Windows|[Microsoft Terminal](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n0dx20hk701?hl=en-GB&gl=CA)|
|MacOS|[Terminal](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/terminal/apd5265185d-f365-44cb-8b09-71a064a42125/mac)|
