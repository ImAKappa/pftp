# 2025-02-09

I TA for an intro computer science course geared towards non-compsci majors.
I notice that students struggle the most with:

- Breaking down problems into a series of steps
- Jumping too quick into trying to write code without first understanding the problem
- Misunderstand bits about the syntax
- Forget about operator precendence
- Have difficulties understanding the call stack and stack frames (which I won't cover here)
