# Dec 9, 2025

Attended a beginner Python workshop hosted by Bailey Ng (from my MScAC program).
Their materials were very organized!

Most of the audience did not have any programming experience.

I also wanted to get more insight into questions from beginners.

Some participants had experience in other languages, like Python.

Teaching habits I like:

- Explain to people when to use what, and how to decide between different options for functions or data types
- Draw connections to things people know ("pandas is like tidyverse, is like Excel, is like SQL")

They were ambitious and also demonstrated some machine learning :0

They used VSCode Codespaces to demonstrate and host code, but accidentally kept swiping "back" in the browser.

- While it's easy to learn, I think it's more useful for people to have an editor on their own system
